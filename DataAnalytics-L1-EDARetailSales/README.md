🛍️ Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship — Task 1, Level 1


---

📌 Project Overview

This project performs an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on an e-commerce retail sales dataset to uncover patterns and insights related to customer demographics, product categories, sales performance, shipping status, and purchasing behaviour.

The analysis transforms raw retail transaction data into meaningful business insights that can support better decision-making and help identify trends in customer purchasing patterns and product performance.


---

🎯 Project Objectives

Explore and understand the retail sales dataset.

Clean and prepare the data for analysis.

Analyse customer demographics and purchasing behaviour.

Examine sales performance across product categories.

Identify trends in sales and order activity.

Analyse shipping status across transactions.

Identify patterns and relationships between numerical variables.

Present findings through clear and informative visualizations.



---

📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains 1,000 e-commerce transactions and includes information about customers, products, sales, shipping, and order dates.

Column	Description

Customer ID	Unique identifier for each customer
Gender	Customer gender
Region	Customer region
Age	Customer age
Product Name	Name of the purchased product
Category	Product category
Unit Price	Price per unit
Quantity	Number of units purchased
Total Price	Total value of the purchase
Shipping Fee	Shipping cost
Shipping Status	Current status of the shipment
Order Date	Date of the transaction

🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset was inspected and cleaned before performing the analysis.

The following steps were carried out:

Checked the dataset structure and data types.

Identified missing values.

Handled missing values in Region, Age, and Shipping Status.

Checked for duplicate records.

Reviewed categorical and numerical variables.

Converted Order Date into an appropriate date format.

Verified the cleaned dataset before analysis.


After cleaning, missing values were successfully addressed, leaving the dataset ready for exploratory analysis.


---

🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis

The analysis focused on several areas of the retail business.

📈 Sales Trends

Sales performance was examined over time using the order dates to identify changes and patterns in purchasing activity.

🛍️ Category Performance

Revenue was compared across product categories to identify the categories generating the highest sales.

👥 Customer Demographics

Customer characteristics such as age and gender were analysed to understand the composition of the customer base.

🚚 Shipping Status

Shipping statuses were analysed to understand the distribution of delivered, in-transit, returned, and other orders.

🔗 Correlation Analysis

A correlation heatmap was used to examine relationships between numerical variables such as age, unit price, quantity, total price, and shipping fee.


---

💡 Key Insights

The analysis produced several important findings:

Electronics generated the highest revenue among the product categories.

Wearables and Accessories generated considerably lower revenue compared with Electronics.

Customers in the 56–65 age group represented the largest age segment.

Customers under 18 represented the smallest age group.

Male customers recorded the highest purchasing activity in the dataset.

The dataset showed different shipping patterns across Delivered, In Transit, Returned, and Unknown statuses.

Sales and purchasing behaviour varied across customer and product characteristics.
